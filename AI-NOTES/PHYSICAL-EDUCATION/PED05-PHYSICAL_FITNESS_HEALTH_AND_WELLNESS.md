# Chapter 5: Physical Fitness, Health and Wellness

## Physical Fitness

**Definition:** The ability to carry out daily tasks without getting tired, while still having enough energy left for emergencies.

Physical fitness has two types of components:

| Health-Related Fitness | Skill-Related Fitness |
| ---------------------- | --------------------- |
| Cardiovascular endurance | Speed |
| Muscular strength | Power |
| Muscular endurance | Agility |
| Flexibility | Balance |
| Body composition | Coordination |

### I. Health-Related Components

| Component | Meaning |
| --------- | ------- |
| **Cardiovascular endurance** | Physical ability of an individual to take part in aerobic exercise for a prolonged period of time |
| **Muscular strength** | Important component of health-related fitness; the maximum force a muscle can exert in a single contraction |
| **Muscular endurance** | A muscle's ability to perform repeated contractions over a period of time without fatigue |
| **Flexibility** | Range of movement of a joint |
| **Body composition** | Percentage of lean body mass (bones, tendons, muscles, water, ligaments, organs, etc.) in comparison to body fat |

### II. Skill-Related Components

| Component | Meaning |
| --------- | ------- |
| **Speed** | Ability to perform a movement of the same pattern at a faster rate |
| **Power** | Ability to overcome resistance with high speed and endurance |
| **Agility** | Ability to change direction and posture, and to move the limbs and other body parts quickly, efficiently and effectively during physical activity |
| **Balance** | Maintenance of equilibrium while moving or in a stationary state |
| **Coordination** | Ability of the nervous system and muscles to work together to perform smooth, accurate and controlled body movements |

---

## Wellness

**Definition:** Optimal functioning of the body and creative adaptation to the environment that involves the whole person.

### Components (Dimensions) of Wellness

1. Physical
2. Emotional
3. Mental
4. Social
5. Environmental
6. Occupational
7. Spiritual
